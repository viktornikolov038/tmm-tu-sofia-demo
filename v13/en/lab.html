Interactive lab

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Five small tools you can touch: gear trains, a four-bar you drag with the mouse, a cam, degrees of freedom and a short quiz.

The models are simplified teaching illustrations, not engineering calculations.

Degrees of freedom

How many input motions does a mechanism need to move unambiguously? Grübler’s formula answers that.

1

Grübler’s formula for planar mechanisms: F = 3(n − 1) − 2p₅ − p₄. It gives the degrees of freedom from the structure only; special dimensions are not considered.
