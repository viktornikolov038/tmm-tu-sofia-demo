Research activity

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Research in the department covers the synthesis and analysis of mechanisms, robotics, machine dynamics and gearing.

Main directions

Table 1 — Research directions
№DirectionContent
1Synthesis of mechanismsDesign of linkages, cams and gears whose points follow prescribed paths.
2Robotics and mechatronicsManipulators, parallel structures and smart drives.
3Dynamics of machinesBalancing, vibration and transient regimes.
4Gears and reducersGeometry, strength and efficiency of spur, planetary and cycloidal gearing.

Forms of realisation

  • publications in Bulgaria and abroad;
  • participation in international projects;
  • contracts with companies;
  • patents and inventions;
  • developments implemented in industry with economic and social effect;
  • consulting for companies.
